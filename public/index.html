🧭 Intelligent Routing Engines & Algorithms

Understand the four machine learning algorithms powering Bharat Express route optimization.

Algorithm 01

Nearest Neighbour Heuristic

Calculates the greedy shortest path by picking the closest unvisited stop.

When Used: When fewer than 4 pending stops remain in a compact sector.
Computation Time: < 5 milliseconds
Advantage: Instantaneous dispatch for local neighborhood drops.
Algorithm 02

Time Window Priority (Urgency)

Sorts stops based on strict SLA deadlines, prioritizing deliveries approaching promise times.

When Used: Rush hours (10:00–12:00 and 18:00–20:00) and express perishables.
Computation Time: < 15 milliseconds
Advantage: Eliminates late deliveries and customer SLA penalties.
Algorithm 03

Weather & Hazard Clustering

Clusters couriers within high-density micro-zones to minimize travel during adverse conditions.

When Used: Heavy rain, localized flooding (Minto Bridge / Dhaula Kuan), or severe smog.
Computation Time: < 25 milliseconds
Advantage: Keeps couriers safe by avoiding hazardous highway stretches.
Algorithm 04 · Default Engine

Hybrid Adaptive Optimizer

Blends spatial proximity, historical traffic graphs, and live driver ratings into a unified cost score.

When Used: Standard daily operations across Delhi NCR and Indian corridors.
Computation Time: < 35 milliseconds
Advantage: Delivers 24.8% fuel savings while honoring all delivery time promises.

💡 Knowledge Guide: FastTrack Routing vs Customer Locked Paths

During checkout, if a user chooses "Customer sets own route", they can lock specific via-points (e.g. Ring Road via India Gate). When dispatch runs:

  • Locked routes are strictly isolated from AI alterations. The driver's navigation device forces compliance with customer-specified waypoints.
  • Unlocked orders are automatically rearranged by the Hybrid Adaptive Optimizer to maximize van fuel efficiency.
